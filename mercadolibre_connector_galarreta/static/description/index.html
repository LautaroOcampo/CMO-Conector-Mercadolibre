Mercado Libre Connector for Odoo

Manage your Mercado Libre operation entirely from Odoo

✅ Odoo 19 Ready 🇦🇷 Argentina & LATAM ⚡ Real-time webhooks 🧾 Auto-invoicing

External service required

This module integrates Odoo with the Mercado Libre API. An active Mercado Libre seller account and a developer application (App ID / Client Secret) are required. Authorization is handled via OAuth 2.0 — your credentials are stored only in your own Odoo database.

Why are Mercado Libre & Odoo
better together — if they are connected?

🏪

Your store, your rules

Manage listings, prices and stock for Mercado Libre directly from Odoo. No more switching between platforms.

🧾

Invoicing on autopilot

Every Mercado Libre sale generates an Odoo invoice automatically — including correct invoice type (A/B) based on the buyer's tax status.

Stock always accurate

Stock updates in Odoo instantly reflect on Mercado Libre listings. Automatic pause/resume rules keep you protected from overselling.

How it works

1
Install and open Mercado Libre Configuration in Odoo

A dedicated Mercado Libre menu appears in the Sales app.

2
Enter your App ID and Client Secret

From your developer app at developers.mercadolibre.com.

3
Authorize with one click via OAuth 2.0

Tokens are stored in your database and refreshed automatically.

4
Import your listings and link them to Odoo products by SKU

Auto-matching by SKU or manual assignment. Full variant support.

5
Enable webhooks — orders, cancellations and invoices are handled automatically

Every sale triggers a sales order, delivery and invoice in Odoo with zero manual work.

What gets synchronized

The most complete Mercado Libre integration available for Odoo.

📦 Listings & Products

  • ✓ Import and manage Mercado Libre listings
  • ✓ Publish Odoo products to Mercado Libre
  • ✓ Sync prices (manual or automatic)
  • ✓ Auto-link listings to Odoo products by SKU
  • ✓ Full variant and attribute support

📊 Stock

  • ✓ Automatic stock sync on every Odoo move
  • ✓ Auto-pause listings at minimum stock
  • ✓ Auto-reactivate when stock is back
  • ✓ Mercado Libre Full (fulfillment) support
  • ✓ Configurable rules per listing

🛒 Orders & Sales

  • ✓ Real-time order import via webhooks
  • ✓ Pack (multi-item cart) support
  • ✓ Customer records created automatically
  • ✓ B2B detection with CUIT lookup
  • ✓ Cancellation handling with credit notes

🧾 Invoicing

  • ✓ Automatic invoice generation per sale
  • ✓ Invoice type A/B based on buyer tax status
  • ✓ Selling fees recorded as separate accounting entry
  • ✓ Fallback product for unmatched SKUs
  • ✓ Multiple Mercado Libre accounts per Odoo instance

Data & privacy

Once authorized, the connector exchanges only the data required for marketplace operations — listings, variants, stock levels, orders, and the buyer and shipping details provided by Mercado Libre. All credentials and tokens are stored exclusively in your own Odoo database. No activation key. No third-party server. No lock-in.
